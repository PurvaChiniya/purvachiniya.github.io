I am an Applied Scientist at Amazon Ads, where I work on agents.

My research spans LLM security, multimodal AI, audio-visual perception, and robust speech recognition. Previously I was an AI Research Intern at the Sentient Foundation, where I built the orchestration layer for Open Deep Search.

I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Maryland, College Park and a B.Tech. from IIT Roorkee.
